About the game

Carve a rift through a living city and swallow it whole.

Start as a crack in the pavement that can barely take a traffic cone. Eat, grow, and by the end you are pulling whole towers, buses and a cruise liner into the dark. Nothing is sucked in by a script: when the ground under something is gone, it falls.

  • Three districts and 21 stages: Resort Coast, Cargo Port and Grand Carnival
  • Four ways to play: Campaign, Classic, Endless and Daily
  • Magnet, Sprint, Freeze and Double Score power-ups
  • Eleven rift skins, from Ember to Void
  • No account, no sign-in, plays offline

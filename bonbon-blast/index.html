About the game

A bright, friendly match-3 space adventure with one big difference: no lives, no timers, no waiting. Ever.

Join a cheerful little robot on a trip across the galaxy. Match four for a striped blaster that clears a whole row, five for a Rainbow Bomb, and combine specials for huge chain reactions.

  • 80 levels across four worlds: Rocket Bay, Crystal Falls, Moon Mines and Star Factory
  • Striped blasters, bombs, Rainbow Bombs and giant combos
  • Shields, ice, metal blocks, purple goo and gift boxes
  • Daily rewards, weekly challenges and win streaks
  • In English and Arabic

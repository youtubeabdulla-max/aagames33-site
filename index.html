About the studio

AA Games is the independent game studio of Abdulla Alhashmi. We make bright, easy-to-learn games for phones and tablets: the kind you can pick up for one quick round, or play for an hour.

From a classic carrom board with a cartoon crew to a colouring book for little kids, every game is made and looked after by us. Questions, bug reports and ideas for new skins all go to the same inbox.

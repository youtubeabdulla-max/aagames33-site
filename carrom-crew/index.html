About the game

The classic board game of flicks and pockets, in a bright cartoon world with a crew of twenty original characters.

Slide your striker along the baseline, pull back to aim, let go and watch the discs fly. Pocket the red Queen, then cover her with one of your own discs on the next strike: you can't win without her.

  • Three ways to play: Carrom, Points and Pool
  • Rookie, Pro and Champion computer opponents
  • Online matches, plus private rooms to play a friend with a room code
  • Friends, leaderboards and emote stickers
  • Characters, strikers and boards to collect

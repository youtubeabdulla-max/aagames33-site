About the game

A picture game for small children. ColorLand asks for one part of a picture: a wing, a wheel, a roof, a petal. Your child finds it, taps it, colours it in and learns what it is called.

Then the whole picture is theirs to colour however they like. Everything is included from the first launch: no ads and nothing to buy inside the app.

  • 54 hand-drawn pictures, all unlocked
  • Every part named in nine languages
  • Picture mysteries: small puzzles built from the pictures themselves
  • Tap-to-fill colouring, brushes, rainbows, stickers and a magic wand
  • Big buttons and no reading needed
